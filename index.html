# BoardSync — Privacy Policy & Support Last updated: September 2026 ## 1. Privacy Policy BoardSync is designed with user privacy in mind. * **Personal Data:** BoardSync does not collect, sell, or monetize your personal information. * **App Data & Syncing:** Any boards, tasks, entries, or data you create or manage within BoardSync remain on your device (or in your private personal iCloud storage if iCloud sync is enabled). We do not transmit or store your board content on external proprietary servers. * **Third-Party Tracking:** BoardSync does not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking cookies, or data brokers. * **Data Retention:** Because we do not run centralized accounts or external user databases, we hold no user data. ## Third-Party AI Processing (Google Gemini) To provide intelligent board organization and processing features, BoardSync transmits certain user-provided content (such as board text or prompts) to the **Google Gemini API**. * **Purpose:** This data is transmitted solely in real-time to generate responses and provide core app functionality. * **Privacy & Security:** Data sent to the Gemini API is processed in accordance with Google's API service terms. We do not use your inputs or generated content to train external public models, and we do not sell your content to third parties. * **Third-Party Policy:** For details on how Google handles API data, please review the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). ## 2.
