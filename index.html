# BoardSync — Privacy Policy & Support Last updated: September 2026 ## 1. Privacy Policy BoardSync is designed with user privacy in mind. * **Personal Data:** BoardSync does not collect, sell, or monetize your personal information. * **App Data & Syncing:** Any boards, tasks, entries, or data you create or manage within BoardSync remain on your device (or in your private personal iCloud storage if iCloud sync is enabled). We do not transmit or store your board content on external proprietary servers. * **Third-Party Tracking:** BoardSync does not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking cookies, or data brokers. * **Data Retention:** Because we do not run centralized accounts or external user databases, we hold no user data. ## 2.
