BoardSync

Privacy Policy & Support — Last updated: September 2026

1. Privacy Policy

BoardSync is designed to respect and protect your privacy.

  • Personal Data: BoardSync does not collect, sell, rent, or monetize your personal information.
  • App Data & Storage: Any boards, tasks, entries, or notes you create remain stored locally on your device (or within your personal Apple iCloud storage if enabled). We do not store your boards on external proprietary servers.
  • Tracking & Advertising: BoardSync contains no third-party advertising SDKs, tracking cookies, or data brokers.
  • Data Retention: Because we do not operate centralized accounts or proprietary user databases, we hold no user data.

Third-Party AI Processing (Google Gemini)

To provide intelligent board organization and processing features, BoardSync transmits certain user-provided content (such as board text or prompts) to the Google Gemini API.

  • Purpose: This data is transmitted solely in real-time to generate responses and provide core app functionality.
  • Privacy & Security: Data sent to the Gemini API is processed in accordance with Google's API service terms. We do not use your inputs or generated content to train external public models, and we do not sell your content to third parties.
  • Third-Party Policy: For details on how Google handles API data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.
